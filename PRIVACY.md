# Privacy Policy for masterG

**Last Updated:** 2024

## Our Commitment to Privacy

masterG is built with privacy as a core principle. We believe that browser extensions should enhance your experience without compromising your privacy.

## Data Collection

### What We DO NOT Collect
- ❌ Personal information (name, email, address, etc.)
- ❌ Browsing history
- ❌ Website content
- ❌ Search queries
- ❌ Cookies or tracking data
- ❌ IP addresses
- ❌ Device identifiers
- ❌ Usage analytics
- ❌ Any data that could identify you

### What We Store Locally
The following data is stored **only on your device** using Chrome's local storage API:

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Theme preference | To remember your dark/light/default choice |
| Installation date | For version management purposes |
| Extension version | To manage updates |

This data:
- Never leaves your device
- Is not transmitted to any server
- Is not shared with any third party
- Can be deleted at any time by removing the extension

## Permissions Explained

### `storage`
- **What it does:** Allows saving your preferences locally
- **Why we need it:** To remember your theme choice
- **Privacy impact:** None - data stays on your device

### `activeTab`
- **What it does:** Allows interaction with the current tab when you click the extension
- **Why we need it:** To apply theme changes to the current page
- **Privacy impact:** None - we only modify visual styling

## Third-Party Services

masterG does not use any third-party services, analytics, or tracking tools.

## Data Transmission

masterG does not transmit any data to external servers. All functionality operates entirely within your browser.

## Open Source

Our code is open source and available for review on GitHub. You can verify our privacy practices by examining the source code.

**GitHub:** [https://github.com/Vibe-coder-ss/masterG](https://github.com/Vibe-coder-ss/masterG)

> **Note:** All links and configuration values are centralized in `config.js` for easy maintenance.

## Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:
- **View:** Check stored data in Chrome's developer tools
- **Delete:** Remove the extension to delete all stored data
- **Modify:** Change preferences anytime through the extension popup

## Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this privacy policy, we will:
1. Update the "Last Updated" date
2. Notify users through the extension update notes
3. Continue to uphold our commitment to privacy
